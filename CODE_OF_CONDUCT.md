# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project develops public-interest standards for AI and quantum computing.
Its legitimacy depends on participation that is diverse, respectful, and
honest. We are committed to providing a harassment-free, constructive
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, discipline, or
affiliation.

## Expected Behavior

- Engage in good faith; critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome diverse and dissenting perspectives, including from affected parties.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest (financial, organizational, personal).
- Respect confidentiality and security constraints where they legitimately apply.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sustained disruption, intimidation, or retaliation against good-faith
  contributors, reviewers, or whistleblowers.
- Attempts to capture the standard for narrow interests by suppressing review,
  evidence, or dissent.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be reported to the maintainers via a private channel (e.g., a
confidential issue or the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`). Maintainers will
review reports promptly, protect reporters from retaliation, and may take any
action they deem appropriate, from a warning to removal from project spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant
(v2.1, https://www.contributor-covenant.org) and the corpus's own governance
values (`01_governance/governance_model.md`, `01_governance/independent_ethics_review.md`).
