# International Alignment (Working Notes)

## Purpose

Keep the corpus legible to regulators and standards bodies by maintaining crosswalks to widely recognized frameworks.

## Ethical Mapping

- `A4 Trustworthiness`: clarity and interoperability
- `A6 Participation`: alignment supports multi-jurisdiction adoption

## Approach

This document is intentionally non-normative. It tracks:

- which external frameworks a given standard resembles
- where this corpus introduces novel requirements (e.g., “right to non-inference”)
- potential conflicts and how to resolve them
